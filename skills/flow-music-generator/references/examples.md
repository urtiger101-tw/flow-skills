# Flow Music 提示詞範例

請依用途改寫時長、語言和曲風，並以 UI 顯示的設定為準。範例沒有實際送出生成。

## 1. 旁白下的純配樂

```text
Create an instrumental underscore for a Taiwanese history documentary, about 90 seconds. Warm felt piano, restrained bowed strings, subtle low percussion, around 80 BPM. Begin quietly, build gentle curiosity in the middle, then end with a clean, soft resolution. Keep the midrange uncluttered so spoken narration stays clear. Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics, no choir, no abrupt ending.
```

檢查：實際長度、無人聲、前中後段有變化，配樂不遮蓋旁白。

## 2. 有人聲的繁體中文歌曲

```text
Create an upbeat city-pop song with natural Taiwanese Mandarin vocals and Traditional Chinese lyrics about returning home on a rainy evening. Female lead voice, bright electric piano, clean bass, soft guitar, steady 100 BPM. Structure: short intro, verse, chorus, second verse, final chorus, clear ending. Keep pronunciation natural and do not insert English lyrics unless requested.
```

檢查：人聲語言、歌詞字形與意義、節奏、結尾；若文字不準，針對歌詞或段落修訂，不直接聲稱符合要求。
